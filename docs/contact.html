Opportunities and contact

I welcome informal enquiries from postdoctoral and postgraduate researchers who would like to join the group and can attract their own funding, for example EU fellowships, international PhD scholarships or visiting positions.

If your interests align with the group's current research, I'm happy to help develop an application. If you're unsure, just ask: my research interests are broad.
